Summary

ArcLink is a Wear OS spatial-logic puzzle game published by aminhanifm. The game works entirely offline and does not require a developer-operated account. The app does not collect or transmit personal information or gameplay data. Information needed to provide progress, statistics, daily puzzles, saved games, and preferences is stored only in the app's private storage on the user's watch.

Information stored on the device

ArcLink may store the following information locally on the watch:

  • whether the tutorial has been completed;
  • Journey progress, unlocked levels, earned stars, best move counts, and best completion times;
  • an unfinished puzzle, including its level, ring positions, move and hint counts, mode, and start time;
  • Daily Link completion dates, best moves, completion totals, and streaks;
  • gameplay statistics, including solved puzzles, rotations, hints, stars, fastest times, and moves above the minimum; and
  • haptic feedback, reduced motion, high contrast, and abandon-confirmation preferences.

This information is not associated with a developer account or sent to the developer. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for ArcLink's app data.

Data collection and transmission

ArcLink does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or use personal information. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, tracking, social, account, or cloud-sync SDK. It does not communicate with the developer or any third-party server.

Google Play distribution

Google Play processes the download, installation, licensing, refund, and, where applicable, purchase activity used to distribute ArcLink. The app itself does not receive or process payment-card details and contains no in-app purchase or subscription system.

Google may provide the developer with sales, order, financial, and device compatibility reports through Google Play Console for distribution, support, refunds, tax, and fraud-prevention purposes. These records are created and controlled by Google Play, not collected or transmitted by the ArcLink app. Google's handling of this information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions and device access

ArcLink requests no Android runtime permissions. Optional haptic feedback is provided locally through standard Wear OS system feedback and can be disabled in the game settings.

The app has no internet access and does not access location, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, health information, nearby devices, notifications, or advertising identifiers.

Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not receive ArcLink gameplay data or personal information and therefore does not share or sell it. ArcLink has no third-party advertising or analytics partners.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on the watch while ArcLink is installed. The in-app Reset Journey action clears Journey progress and any unfinished puzzle while preserving settings and statistics. The Reset statistics action clears counters, streaks, and fastest times while preserving settings and Journey progress.

To delete all ArcLink data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Because ArcLink sends no data to the developer, there is no remote ArcLink account or gameplay database to delete. Google retains and deletes Play distribution and purchase records according to its own policies, account controls, and legal obligations.

Security

ArcLink uses Android app-private storage for its local data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot transmit that data over the internet. Users should still protect their watch and Google account with the security controls available on their devices.

Children's privacy

ArcLink is a general-audience puzzle game. The app does not collect personal information from children or adults. A parent or guardian with a question can use the contact details below.
